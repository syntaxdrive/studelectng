01

Overview — What is StudElect?

StudElect is a multi-tenant digital election platform built specifically for Nigerian tertiary institutions. It allows student organisations — SUG, NESA, NACOS, faculty associations, departmental associations, and more — to conduct fully cryptographic, constitutionally compliant elections entirely online.

As an ELCOM (Electoral Commission) administrator, you are the operator of your organisation's election. StudElect gives you the tools to manage the entire process — from building the voter roll to publishing verified results — without needing any technical background.

Key Concepts

Term What It Means
Institution Your university or polytechnic (e.g. University of Ibadan, UNILAG)
Organisation Your student body within that institution (e.g. NESA, NACOS, SUG)
Voter Roll The official list of students eligible to vote in your election
Accreditation The process by which a voter confirms their identity and receives a one-time voting token
PIN A 6-digit one-time code generated per student for accreditation. Students enter this in the voter booth.
Ballot Token A cryptographic key issued after accreditation that authorises exactly one vote. Cannot be reused.
Election Status PENDING LIVE PAUSED ENDED
🔒
Security Architecture

No two votes can be linked back to a specific student. Ballot selections are cryptographically decoupled from voter identity. Even the ELCOM administrator cannot see who voted for whom — only the aggregate count per candidate.

Before You Begin — What You Need

Before running an election on StudElect, make sure the following are ready:

  • Your login credentials from the StudElect platform administrator (email and password)
  • A laptop or desktop computer with a modern browser (Chrome, Firefox, Edge, or Safari)
  • A stable internet connection
  • Your voter roll in Excel format (.xlsx) — matric numbers, full names, departments, and academic levels
  • Your list of positions (offices) to be contested
  • Your list of cleared/screened candidates per position
  • The agreed election date(s) and time window
  • Confirmation from the platform administrator that your organisation's license is ACTIVE
⚠
Important

The election cannot go LIVE if your organisation's payment license is not active. Contact your StudElect platform representative if your election is being blocked by a payment hold.

Logging In for the First Time

Your login link is specific to your institution. It follows this format:

https://studelect.ng/{your-institution-slug}/admin

For example, if your institution is University of Ibadan and your organisation is NESA, your admin panel is at:

https://studelect.ng/ui/admin

Alternatively, you can go to the general login page at https://studelect.ng/admin/login and enter your credentials there.

1

Open your institution's admin URL

Navigate to studelect.ng/{institution}/admin in your browser. You will be redirected to the login page if you are not already signed in.

2

Enter your email and password

Use the credentials provided to you by the StudElect platform administrator. Your password is case-sensitive.

3

Click "Sign In to Admin Panel"

You will be authenticated and redirected to your ELCOM dashboard for your specific organisation.

4

Verify you are in the correct organisation

Check the top of the dashboard — it should show your institution name, organisation name, and your role as "ELCOM Commissioner". If it is wrong, sign out and contact the platform administrator.

🚨
Security — Do Not Share

Never share your admin password with anyone, including other ELCOM members. If you believe your credentials are compromised, contact the platform administrator immediately to reset them.

Organization Branding & Logo Upload (DP)

In the top navigation header of your ELCOM dashboard, locate the camera icon beside your organisation badge. Clicking this allows you to upload your official departmental, faculty, or union logo (PNG or JPEG, up to 2.5MB).

  • Strict Isolation: Your logo is strictly scoped to your organisation and stored securely. It will never overwrite or leak into other campus organizations.
  • Automatic Booth Branding: Once uploaded, your logo automatically brands both your administrator control room and the student-facing voting booth at studelect.ng/{institution}/{org}.

ELCOM Public Support Desk

Under the Election Settings tab, you can configure your committee's official support channels: ELCOM Chairman's Name, Official WhatsApp Help Desk number, and Support Email.

These details are rendered live at the base of the student voting portal so that accredited students who have questions, need clarification on their departmental dues, or require accreditation assistance can contact your committee directly in real time.

1-Click Disaster Recovery (Backup & Restore)

Under the Settings tab, StudElect provides a built-in Disaster Recovery Suite:

  • Export Election Backup (JSON): Downloads a comprehensive, timestamped snapshot of your entire election state — including your rules, elective offices, nominated candidates with profile photos, cast encrypted ballots, voter deduplication ledger, and commissioner credentials. Keep this file downloaded to an external drive or secure cloud storage.
  • Restore Election Backup: In the unlikely event of an accidental modification or device emergency, you can restore your election snapshot with a single click. The restore process strictly merges and replaces data for your organisation only, preserving other campus elections untouched.
05

Setting Up Election Rules & Registration Locks

Before accrediting voters or launching polls, configure your operational parameters in the Election Settings tab. These rules govern access security, timeline enforcement, and transparency.

1

Set Official Election Title & Description

Provide your constitutional election title (e.g., "NESA General Elections 2025/2026 Academic Session") and any preamble instructions for voters.

2

Configure Voting Hours & Auto-Pause Closing Timer

Specify official poll opening and closing timestamps. Outside these hours, the voter booth automatically displays a polite countdown or "Voting Closed" screen even if the admin state is LIVE.

3

Enforce Constitutional Eligibility Criteria

Configure accreditation prerequisites: Departmental Dues Payment (blocks students with unpaid dues) and Disciplinary Standing (Good Standing vs. Suspended).

4

Manage Student Registration Lock ("Open" vs "Closed")

Control whether new students can self-register on the voter booth. When set to Open, students can register and obtain PINs. When set to Closed, new sign-ups are barred.

5

Configure Results Visibility (Sealed vs. Live)

Choose when candidate tallies become public: Hidden / Sealed (recommended during voting to prevent bandwagon effects), Live Stream (tallies visible in real time), or Post-Election (automatically unsealed upon reaching ENDED status).

6

Save Election Rules

Click "Save Election Rules". All changes take effect immediately across all active voter sessions.

🔒
Crucial Rule: Closing Registration Never Blocks Existing Voters

When you toggle Student Registration to Closed, you are strictly preventing new unverified accounts from signing up.

Existing registered students are NOT locked out! Any student who has already registered or was imported on the voter roll can log in with their Matriculation Number and PIN and cast their ballot without any interruption. Closing registration is standard best practice right before opening the polls.

📍
Best Practice: Seal Results During Voting

Keep results Hidden / Sealed until the official close of voting. Broadcasting candidate margins while voting is open can trigger bandwagon voting, candidate voter suppression, or public anxiety. Unseal results only after voting is formally declared ENDED.

Building Your Voter Roll & Online Student Self-Registration

The voter roll is the authoritative register of students eligible to participate in your election. StudElect provides two flexible methods to assemble your voter roll: bulk Excel roster upload and direct student online self-registration.

Method A — Bulk Excel Roster Upload (.xlsx)

If your department or faculty executive already possesses an official roster of registered students, you can import all eligible voters in seconds using an Excel spreadsheet.

Column Field Name Required? Example
A Matriculation Number Yes 200404/BE/EEE/001 or 214950
B Full Name Yes Adebayo Chinonso Felix
C Department Yes Economics
D Academic Level Yes 300
E Email Address (NDPR) Yes adebayo@student.ui.edu.ng
F Phone / WhatsApp Optional 08012345678
G Dues Paid (Yes/No) Optional Yes
1

Prepare your Excel spreadsheet

Row 1 must contain headers. Student records begin on row 2. Remove any merged cells or formatting formulas.

2

Click "Voter Roll" → "Upload Roster"

Select your .xlsx file. StudElect previews the first rows and validates matric number uniqueness.

3

Confirm Import & Auto-Generate PINs

Click "Import". The platform creates each student account and automatically generates a unique organization-prefixed PIN (e.g. NES-4892) for every voter.

Method B — Online Student Self-Registration ("New Voter? Get PIN")

In many university elections, an up-to-date departmental Excel sheet is unavailable. StudElect provides an automated, zero-stress self-registration portal built directly into your voter booth.

1

Direct Students to Your Voter Booth URL

Share your organization's booth link: https://studelect.ng/{institution}/{organization} (e.g. https://studelect.ng/ui/nesa or https://studelect.ng/ui/renarsa).

2

Student Clicks "New Voter? Get PIN"

On the voter booth home page, students who do not yet have credentials click the "New Voter? Get PIN" tab to launch the registration modal.

3

Student Completes Registration Details

The student inputs their Matriculation Number, Full Legal Name, Academic Level (100–500), Department, compulsory Email Address, and optional Phone/WhatsApp Number.

4

Instant Organization-Prefixed PIN Issuance

Upon submission, StudElect instantly generates an 8-character PIN prefixed by the organization abbreviation (e.g. NES-4892 for NESA or REN-7104 for RENARSA). The modal provides a 1-click "Copy PIN" button with a confirmation notice.

📣
Why Email is Compulsory (NDPR Compliance)

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Regulation (NDPR) and electoral integrity standards, every self-registered voter must provide a valid email address. This email address serves as the verified audit destination for cryptographic ballot receipts, dispute audits, and election announcements.

Self-Service PIN Recovery ("Lookup My PIN")

During elections, students frequently forget or misplace their PINs. StudElect eliminates admin bottlenecks with the "Lookup My PIN" self-service tool:

  • On the voter booth accreditation screen, any registered student can click "Lookup My PIN".
  • They enter their registered Matriculation Number.
  • The system verifies their enrollment and instantly displays their existing PIN on screen with a "Copy PIN" shortcut.
  • ELCOM administrators do not need to manually field hundreds of "I lost my PIN" WhatsApp messages on election day!

Locking Student Self-Registration Before Polls Open

Once your registration window has elapsed (e.g., midnight before election day):

  • Navigate to Election Settings on your ELCOM dashboard.
  • Toggle "Student Registration" from Open to Closed.
  • Click "Save Election Rules".

Once closed, new visitors to the voting booth will see a clear notice that registration has ended. Crucially: All existing registered voters can continue to log in and vote uninterrupted throughout the voting window!

07

Managing Student Eligibility & PIN Recovery

The Voter Roll tab provides complete oversight over every registered student, enabling ELCOM to enforce dues requirements, manage disciplinary standing, and handle individual student support.

Departmental Dues Payment Clearance

If your association constitution requires departmental dues payment as a voting prerequisite:

1

Search for the Student

Use the real-time search bar in the Voter Roll tab to find the student by Matriculation Number or Name.

2

Toggle Dues Status

Click the dues badge (Paid / Unpaid). The status updates immediately in the database.

3

Student Accredits Instantly

The student can immediately refresh their voter booth browser and proceed past the accreditation gate.

Disciplinary Standing (Active vs. Suspended)

If a student is on academic suspension, disciplinary probation, or otherwise disqualified from voting, click "Deactivate" next to their name. A deactivated student is barred from accreditation even with a valid PIN.

Manual PIN Reset (Admin Override)

While students should first be directed to the "Lookup My PIN" button on the voter booth, ELCOM administrators can also generate a brand-new PIN at any time by clicking "Reset PIN" in the Voter Roll row. A fresh org-prefixed PIN is generated and displayed for you to share directly with the student.

🚨
PIN Confidentiality Rule

Voter PINs are single-use cryptographic credentials. Never paste a list of PINs into public WhatsApp groups or faculty bulletin boards. Only share PINs directly with the verified owner via private communication.

Promoting a Student to ELCOM Commissioner

If you need to assign assistant returning officers, polling agents, or fellow committee members to the admin dashboard, find their record on the voter roll and click "Promote to Admin". Their account will immediately gain administrative access to your organisation's control panel.

Adding Positions & Nominating Candidates (Photos & Manifestos)

StudElect structures your ballot into a clean, two-tier hierarchy: Elective Offices (Positions) and Nominated Candidates. Before launching your election, set up your constitutional offices and nominate screened contestants.

Tier 1 — Create Elective Offices

1

Navigate to the "Candidates" Tab

Open the Candidates tab on your ELCOM dashboard. Click the blue "Add New Elective Office" button.

2

Enter Office Title

Provide the constitutional title, e.g. "Executive President", "Vice President", "General Secretary", "Financial Secretary", "Public Relations Officer (PRO)".

3

Enter Office Description & Responsibilities

Briefly describe the responsibilities of the post. This helps educate voters on the duties of each executive office.

4

Save Elective Office

Click "Save Office". The post is created as a dedicated office card ready for candidate nominations.

Tier 2 — Nominate Candidates (Photos, Monikers & Manifestos)

Once an office card exists, nominate one or more screened candidates for that post:

1

Click "Nominate Candidate" on the Office Card

Locate the target office card and click the "Nominate Candidate" button to open the nomination modal.

2

Enter Candidate Identity Details

Fill in the candidate's Full Legal Name (e.g. Olawale Adebayo Sunday) and their student Matriculation Number.

3

Add Campaign Moniker / Nickname

Enter the candidate's popular campaign moniker (e.g. "Hon. Catalyst", "The Econometrician"). This appears prominently on the digital ballot card alongside their official name so voters immediately recognise their candidate.

4

Upload Candidate Passport / Profile Photo

Use the file selector to upload a clear passport or campaign photograph (JPEG, PNG, or WebP up to 2.5MB). An instant thumbnail preview confirms that the candidate's face is centered and sharp.

5

Enter Campaign Manifesto / Bio Statement

Paste the candidate's summary manifesto or core policy pillars. Voters in the booth can expand and read this statement before casting their vote.

6

Set Screening Status to "Cleared"

Select the ELCOM screening outcome:
• Cleared (green badge): Candidate has passed all constitutional checks and will appear on the student ballot.
• Nominated / Pending (yellow badge): Candidate is awaiting screening; hidden from the student ballot.
• Disqualified (red badge): Candidate failed screening; permanently hidden from the student ballot.

Editing or Removing Candidates

On any candidate card:

  • Click the Pencil (Edit) icon to revise spelling, update the campaign manifesto, or upload a new profile picture.
  • Click the Trash (Delete) icon to remove a withdrawn nominee.
✓
Pre-Flight Audit

Before launching your election LIVE, review each office card. Verify that every cleared candidate displays their high-resolution photo, correct moniker, and full name. Once votes start coming in, candidate lists cannot be modified to ensure cryptographic audit integrity.

Going LIVE — Launching the Election

This is the most important step. Once you set the election to LIVE, the voter booth opens and students can begin accrediting and casting ballots.

Pre-Launch Checklist

  • Election rules are saved (title, voting hours, eligibility, results visibility)
  • Voter roll has been uploaded and verified
  • All eligible students are marked as dues-paid (if required)
  • All positions have been created
  • All candidates have been added and marked as "Cleared"
  • Platform license is ACTIVE (confirm with your StudElect representative)
  • You have tested the voter booth URL and it loads correctly

Setting the Election to LIVE

1

Go to "Election Control" on your dashboard

Find the status control panel. The current status is PENDING.

2

Click "Set to LIVE"

A confirmation dialog will appear. Read it carefully.

3

Confirm the action

Click "Yes, Launch Election". The status changes to LIVE immediately.

4

Share the voter booth URL with students

The voter booth is at studelect.ng/{institution}/{org}. Post this on your official WhatsApp group, notice board, or student portal announcement.

📱
What to Share with Students

Send students this message:

"Dear [Org] members, voting is now open. Visit studelect.ng/{institution}/{org} to cast your ballot. You will need your matriculation number and the 6-digit PIN sent to you. Voting closes at [time]. Your vote is secret and cryptographically secured."

During the Election — Real-Time Monitoring & Free-Tier Eco-Protection

While the election is LIVE, your dashboard provides real-time telemetry updated smoothly without reloading the page.

Metric What It Tells You Action If Concern
Total Registered Number of students currently on your voter roll Should match your department or faculty enrollment expectation
Accredited Students who have passed credential checks and entered the ballot booth If low relative to total, broadcast reminders on official group chats
Ballots Cast Number of successfully encrypted and verified ballots submitted Tracks voter participation velocity in real time
Voter Turnout % Ballots cast as an exact percentage of registered voters Helps ELCOM monitor whether constitutional quorum has been achieved
Live Leaderboard Real-time candidate vote tallies (if results visibility is set to Live) We recommend keeping results Sealed until voting formally ends

Zero-Hiccup Free-Tier Eco-Protection Architecture

During peak election hours, hundreds of students may be concurrently registering, reading manifestos, and casting ballots. StudElect incorporates high-efficiency architectural safeguards specifically engineered to guarantee uninterrupted, hiccup-free voting on database free-tiers (such as Supabase):

  • Zero-DB File Store for Ballot Counts: Live ballot counts are decoupled from high-frequency SQL queries. When ballots are cast, counts are written to an atomic, append-only cryptographic ledger. The real-time telemetry polling reads this ledger without burdening the PostgreSQL database connection pool.
  • 2.5-Second Telemetry Throttle: Admin telemetry endpoints implement an in-memory caching layer with a 2.5-second TTL. Even if multiple ELCOM commissioners keep their dashboards open simultaneously, the server executes at most one refresh cycle per interval.
  • Smart Background Tab Hibernation: If an administrator minimizes their browser window or switches to another tab, polling intervals are automatically halted to conserve bandwidth and execution units. Telemetry catches up instantly the moment the tab is brought back into active view.
  • Ballot Sealing (Bandwagon Mitigation): When results are marked as Sealed in settings, candidate totals are completely withheld from public network payloads, preserving electoral secrecy and reducing bandwidth overhead.

Pausing the Election

If an urgent dispute arises or there is a formal ELCOM resolution to pause voting (e.g., verifying a candidate credential), click "Pause Election" in the Election Control panel.

▐▐
When the Election is PAUSED

The voter booth immediately displays a "Voting Temporarily Suspended" banner. Students cannot accredit or submit new ballots. All existing cast ballots remain intact. You can resume (set back to LIVE) at any moment with a single click.

Handling Student Issues During Voting

Student Problem Your Action
Cannot find matriculation number Check the Voter Roll — they may not have completed self-registration. If registration is still open, send them to the booth to click "New Voter? Get PIN". If closed, you can add them manually.
Lost or forgot their voter PIN Instruct the student to click "Lookup My PIN" on the booth accreditation screen and enter their matric number. Alternatively, click "Reset PIN" on their row in your Voter Roll tab.
Sees "Registration Closed" notice Clarify that new registrations are closed. If they already registered previously, they only need to enter their Matriculation Number and PIN to accredit and vote — existing voters are never blocked!
"Dues not paid" error at accreditation Verify their departmental receipt. If paid, find their record in Voter Roll and toggle dues to Paid. The student can immediately proceed.
"Already voted" error StudElect strictly enforces one-vote-per-matric. Check the audit log to verify the timestamp of their cast ballot receipt. Ballots cannot be duplicated or cast twice.
Student selected wrong candidate accidentally Once a cryptographic ballot is committed to the blockchain-style ledger, it is permanent and irreversible. Advise students to review their selections on the confirmation screen before final submission.

Closing the Election

When voting time is over, you must officially close (end) the election. This is a permanent action — once ended, the election cannot be set back to LIVE.

1

Confirm voting has ended

Make a formal announcement in your student group that voting is closed. Give a 5-minute grace period for students who are mid-session.

2

Go to Election Control

Click "End Election". A confirmation dialog will appear warning you that this action is irreversible.

3

Confirm

The election status changes to ENDED. The voter booth immediately shows "Voting has closed." No further votes can be cast.

4

Note the final vote counts

Take screenshots or download the results immediately. Go to the Results section and record the final tally for each position.

📋
Good Practice

Document the exact time the election ended, the final turnout percentage, and take a screenshot of the Results page. This forms part of your official electoral record and may be required for disputes.

12

Publishing Results

After closing the election, you can publish the results publicly on the platform. Published results are visible to all students at the voter booth URL — no login required.

1

Go to "Results" on your dashboard

You will see the final vote tally per candidate per position, sorted from highest to lowest votes.

2

Review the results carefully

Verify total votes per position equals the number of ballots cast. If there is a discrepancy, check the audit log before publishing.

3

Toggle "Results Visibility" to Public

Click "Make Results Public". A green confirmation will appear. Results are now visible at studelect.ng/{institution}/{org}.

4

Announce the results

Share the results link in your official student channels. Students can verify their ballot on the Verify page using their ballot receipt hash.

Result Verification

Every student who voted received a ballot receipt containing a unique cryptographic hash. Students can go to studelect.ng/{institution}/elections/{electionId}/verify and enter their hash to confirm their vote was counted — without revealing who they voted for. This is part of StudElect's end-to-end verifiability guarantee.

Post-Election — Audit & Record Keeping

After the election ends, StudElect maintains a full cryptographic audit trail. This is important for transparency and for handling any petitions or disputes.

Audit Log

The Audit Log shows every ballot event in the order it occurred. Each entry contains:

Audit Field Description
Timestamp The exact date and time the ballot was cast (to the millisecond)
Ballot Hash A unique cryptographic fingerprint of this specific ballot
Previous Hash The hash of the ballot before this one — forming an unbreakable chain
Voter Token An anonymised token (not the student's matric number) proving this was a valid accredited voter
Chain Integrity A verified/failed indicator showing whether the ballot chain is intact

Downloading Records

Use the Export buttons on the Results and Voter Roll pages to download CSV files of final results and voter participation records. Store these securely as your official electoral archive for the session.

📄
Record Retention

Keep your downloaded results and audit exports for a minimum of one full academic session. These may be required by your institution's Student Affairs office or in the event of an electoral petition.
