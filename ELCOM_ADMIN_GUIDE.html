01

Overview — What is StudElect?

StudElect is a multi-tenant digital election platform built specifically for Nigerian tertiary institutions. It allows student organisations — SUG, NESA, NACOS, faculty associations, departmental associations, and more — to conduct fully cryptographic, constitutionally compliant elections entirely online.

As an ELCOM (Electoral Commission) administrator, you are the operator of your organisation's election. StudElect gives you the tools to manage the entire process — from building the voter roll to publishing verified results — without needing any technical background.

Key Concepts

Term What It Means
Institution Your university or polytechnic (e.g. University of Ibadan, UNILAG)
Organisation Your student body within that institution (e.g. NESA, NACOS, SUG)
Voter Roll The official list of students eligible to vote in your election
Accreditation The process by which a voter confirms their identity and receives a one-time voting token
PIN A 6-digit one-time code generated per student for accreditation. Students enter this in the voter booth.
Ballot Token A cryptographic key issued after accreditation that authorises exactly one vote. Cannot be reused.
Election Status PENDING LIVE PAUSED ENDED
🔒
Security Architecture

No two votes can be linked back to a specific student. Ballot selections are cryptographically decoupled from voter identity. Even the ELCOM administrator cannot see who voted for whom — only the aggregate count per candidate.

Before You Begin — What You Need

Before running an election on StudElect, make sure the following are ready:

  • Your login credentials from the StudElect platform administrator (email and password)
  • A laptop or desktop computer with a modern browser (Chrome, Firefox, Edge, or Safari)
  • A stable internet connection
  • Your voter roll in Excel format (.xlsx) — matric numbers, full names, departments, and academic levels
  • Your list of positions (offices) to be contested
  • Your list of cleared/screened candidates per position
  • The agreed election date(s) and time window
  • Confirmation from the platform administrator that your organisation's license is ACTIVE
⚠
Important

The election cannot go LIVE if your organisation's payment license is not active. Contact your StudElect platform representative if your election is being blocked by a payment hold.

03

Logging In for the First Time

Your login link is specific to your institution. It follows this format:

https://studelect.com.ng/{your-institution-slug}/admin

For example, if your institution is University of Ibadan and your organisation is NESA, your admin panel is at:

https://studelect.com.ng/ui/admin

Alternatively, you can go to the general login page at https://studelect.com.ng/admin/login and enter your credentials there.

1

Open your institution's admin URL

Navigate to studelect.com.ng/{institution}/admin in your browser. You will be redirected to the login page if you are not already signed in.

2

Enter your email and password

Use the credentials provided to you by the StudElect platform administrator. Your password is case-sensitive.

3

Click "Sign In to Admin Panel"

You will be authenticated and redirected to your ELCOM dashboard for your specific organisation.

4

Verify you are in the correct organisation

Check the top of the dashboard — it should show your institution name, organisation name, and your role as "ELCOM Commissioner". If it is wrong, sign out and contact the platform administrator.

🚨
Security — Do Not Share

Never share your admin password with anyone, including other ELCOM members. If you believe your credentials are compromised, contact the platform administrator immediately to reset them.

Navigating the ELCOM Dashboard

The ELCOM dashboard is divided into clear sections. Here is what each area does:

Dashboard Section What You Do Here
Overview / Telemetry See real-time stats — total registered voters, accredited count, ballots cast, turnout percentage
Election Settings Configure your election rules: voting hours, eligibility criteria, results visibility
Voter Roll View all registered students, their eligibility status, dues payment, and PIN management
Candidates Add positions (offices), upload or manually enter candidate details, set screening status
Election Control Change election status: PENDING → LIVE → PAUSED → ENDED
Results View live or final vote tallies per candidate, toggle public visibility of results

05

Setting Up Election Rules

Before adding candidates or uploading voters, configure your election rules. These settings govern how the election will operate.

1

Go to "Election Settings" on your dashboard

Locate the Election Settings or Election Rules panel on your dashboard.

2

Set the Election Title

Give your election a formal name, e.g. "NESA General Elections 2025/2026 Session"

3

Set Voting Hours

Define the opening and closing time for the voter booth. Outside these hours, the booth will show a "Voting Closed" message even if the election status is LIVE.

4

Configure Eligibility Rules

Choose which criteria students must meet to vote. Options typically include: dues payment, active disciplinary standing, and verified registration.

5

Set Results Visibility

Choose when results become public. Options: Hidden (manual release), Live (visible during voting), or Post-Election (visible only after you close the election).

6

Save Settings

Click "Save Election Rules". These can be updated at any time while the election is PENDING. Once LIVE, some settings are locked for integrity.

📍
Pro Tip

Keep results hidden until voting closes. If results are visible while voting is open, it can influence remaining voters and compromise the integrity of the election.

06

Building Your Voter Roll (Uploading Students)

The voter roll is the authoritative list of students who may vote in your election. StudElect accepts voter roll uploads in Excel (.xlsx) format.

Preparing Your Excel File

Your Excel file must have the following columns in this exact order:

Column Field Name Required? Example
A Matriculation Number Yes 200404/BE/EEE/001
B Full Name Yes Adebayo Chinonso Felix
C Department Yes Economics
D Academic Level Yes 300
E Email Optional adebayo@student.ui.edu.ng
F Phone / WhatsApp Optional 08012345678
G Dues Paid (Yes/No) Optional Yes
1

Prepare your Excel file

Ensure row 1 contains column headers. Data starts from row 2. Remove any merged cells, formulas, or special characters in matric numbers.

2

Navigate to "Voter Roll" → "Upload Roster"

Click the upload button and select your Excel file. The system will preview the first few rows for you to confirm.

3

Confirm the upload

Review the preview. If the columns align correctly, click "Import". The system will add all students to your voter roll and generate a unique PIN for each one.

4

Verify the import

After import, the Voter Roll table will show all students. Check the total count matches your expected number. You can add or remove students manually after import.

⚠
Matric Number Formatting

Matric numbers are normalised automatically (punctuation, spacing, and case differences are handled). However, ensure there are no completely wrong matric numbers — students will not be able to find themselves in the system if their matric number is wrong.

Adding Students Manually

If you need to add individual students after the bulk upload, go to Voter Roll → Add Student and fill in the required fields. Each manually added student also receives a generated PIN.

07

Managing Student Eligibility

Not every student on the voter roll may be automatically eligible to vote. StudElect lets you enforce eligibility rules and manage individual student status.

Dues Payment Status

If your election requires dues payment as a condition for voting, you can mark students as dues-paid individually or via bulk update. Students who are not marked as dues-paid will be blocked from accreditation at the voter booth.

1

Go to Voter Roll

You will see each student's current dues status. A red "Unpaid" badge indicates they cannot vote yet.

2

Find the student

Use the search box to find a student by name or matric number.

3

Toggle their dues status

Click "Mark as Paid" (or the dues toggle) next to the student. The change takes effect immediately — they will be able to accredit on their next attempt at the voter booth.

Deactivating a Student

If a student should be excluded from voting entirely (e.g. disciplinary action, withdrawal), you can deactivate their account from the Voter Roll. A deactivated student cannot accredit or vote, even if their matric number is in the system.

Resetting a PIN

If a student lost their PIN or it was issued incorrectly, you can reset it from the Voter Roll by clicking the Reset PIN action next to their record. A new 6-digit PIN is generated instantly. Share it with the student securely — verbally or via direct WhatsApp message. Never post PINs publicly.

🚨
PIN Confidentiality

PINs are personal and single-use. Once a student uses their PIN to accredit, it cannot be used again. Never paste PINs in group chats or broadcast messages — only send each PIN to the individual student it belongs to.

How Students Register Themselves

Students can also self-register at the voter booth by visiting studelect.com.ng/{institution}/{org}. They enter their matric number and other details. The system checks whether they exist in your voter roll. If they do, they are confirmed. If they do not, registration is rejected — which is why your voter roll must be complete before the election goes LIVE.

Promoting a Student to Admin

If you need a student to have ELCOM admin access (e.g. a returning officer who needs to monitor the dashboard), you can promote them from the Voter Roll. Find the student and click "Promote to Admin". The next time they log into the admin panel with their student account, they will have admin-level access.

08

Adding Positions and Candidates

Before the election goes LIVE, you must configure the positions being contested and the candidates running for each position.

Step 1 — Create Positions (Offices)

1

Go to "Candidates" → "Manage Positions"

You will see an empty list initially. Click "Add Position".

2

Enter the position title

E.g. "President", "Vice President", "Secretary General", "Financial Secretary", "PRO", "Senate Representative", etc.

3

Set the number of winners

For most positions this is 1. For positions like "Senators" where multiple candidates win, set the appropriate number.

4

Save and repeat for each position

Add all your positions before moving on to candidates.

Step 2 — Add Candidates

1

Select a position from the list

Click "Add Candidate" under the relevant position.

2

Enter candidate details

Full name, matric number, department, level. Optionally upload a passport photo.

3

Set screening status to "Cleared"

Only candidates marked as Cleared will appear on the ballot. Candidates marked as "Disqualified" or "Pending" will not be shown to voters.

4

Repeat for all candidates across all positions

Double-check spellings and matric numbers. These appear exactly as entered on the voter's ballot.

✓
Final Check

Before going LIVE, click through each position and confirm every cleared candidate appears correctly. Candidates cannot be edited once the first ballot has been cast.

09

Going LIVE — Launching the Election

This is the most important step. Once you set the election to LIVE, the voter booth opens and students can begin accrediting and casting ballots.

Pre-Launch Checklist

  • Election rules are saved (title, voting hours, eligibility, results visibility)
  • Voter roll has been uploaded and verified
  • All eligible students are marked as dues-paid (if required)
  • All positions have been created
  • All candidates have been added and marked as "Cleared"
  • Platform license is ACTIVE (confirm with your StudElect representative)
  • You have tested the voter booth URL and it loads correctly

Setting the Election to LIVE

1

Go to "Election Control" on your dashboard

Find the status control panel. The current status is PENDING.

2

Click "Set to LIVE"

A confirmation dialog will appear. Read it carefully.

3

Confirm the action

Click "Yes, Launch Election". The status changes to LIVE immediately.

4

Share the voter booth URL with students

The voter booth is at studelect.com.ng/{institution}/{org}. Post this on your official WhatsApp group, notice board, or student portal announcement.

📱
What to Share with Students

Send students this message:

"Dear [Org] members, voting is now open. Visit studelect.com.ng/{institution}/{org} to cast your ballot. You will need your matriculation number and the 6-digit PIN sent to you. Voting closes at [time]. Your vote is secret and cryptographically secured."

10

During the Election — Real-Time Monitoring

While the election is LIVE, your dashboard shows real-time statistics updated every few seconds. Here is what to watch:

Metric What It Tells You Action If Concern
Total Registered Number of students on the voter roll Should match your expected eligible count
Accredited Students who have verified their identity and received a ballot token If very low, remind students to accredit before voting closes
Ballots Cast Number of completed votes submitted Monitor for unusual spikes or drops
Voter Turnout % Ballots cast as a percentage of registered voters Low turnout — send reminders via your group channel
Live Leaderboard Current vote counts per candidate (if results visibility is set to Live) Consider setting to Hidden if this may discourage voters

Pausing the Election

If a technical issue arises or there is a formal ELCOM resolution to pause voting (e.g. a candidate dispute), you can pause the election from the Election Control panel.

▐▐
When the Election is PAUSED

The voter booth shows a "Voting Temporarily Suspended" banner. Students cannot accredit or submit new ballots. All data is preserved. You can resume (set back to LIVE) at any time.

Handling Student Issues During Voting

Student Problem Your Action
Cannot find their matric number Check the voter roll — they may not be registered. If they should be eligible, add them manually and share their generated PIN.
PIN not working Go to Voter Roll, find the student, click Reset PIN. Share the new PIN with them directly.
"Dues not paid" error at accreditation If they have paid, go to Voter Roll and toggle their dues status to Paid.
"Already voted" but student says they did not The system has a strict one-vote-per-matric rule. Check the audit log. If there is evidence of accidental double-submission, escalate to the platform administrator.
Student voted for wrong candidate Ballots are final and cannot be reversed. This is standard electoral procedure. The student should have reviewed their choices before submitting.
11

Closing the Election

When voting time is over, you must officially close (end) the election. This is a permanent action — once ended, the election cannot be set back to LIVE.

1

Confirm voting has ended

Make a formal announcement in your student group that voting is closed. Give a 5-minute grace period for students who are mid-session.

2

Go to Election Control

Click "End Election". A confirmation dialog will appear warning you that this action is irreversible.

3

Confirm

The election status changes to ENDED. The voter booth immediately shows "Voting has closed." No further votes can be cast.

4

Note the final vote counts

Take screenshots or download the results immediately. Go to the Results section and record the final tally for each position.

📋
Good Practice

Document the exact time the election ended, the final turnout percentage, and take a screenshot of the Results page. This forms part of your official electoral record and may be required for disputes.

12

Publishing Results

After closing the election, you can publish the results publicly on the platform. Published results are visible to all students at the voter booth URL — no login required.

1

Go to "Results" on your dashboard

You will see the final vote tally per candidate per position, sorted from highest to lowest votes.

2

Review the results carefully

Verify total votes per position equals the number of ballots cast. If there is a discrepancy, check the audit log before publishing.

3

Toggle "Results Visibility" to Public

Click "Make Results Public". A green confirmation will appear. Results are now visible at studelect.com.ng/{institution}/{org}.

4

Announce the results

Share the results link in your official student channels. Students can verify their ballot on the Verify page using their ballot receipt hash.

Result Verification

Every student who voted received a ballot receipt containing a unique cryptographic hash. Students can go to studelect.com.ng/{institution}/elections/{electionId}/verify and enter their hash to confirm their vote was counted — without revealing who they voted for. This is part of StudElect's end-to-end verifiability guarantee.

13

Post-Election — Audit & Record Keeping

After the election ends, StudElect maintains a full cryptographic audit trail. This is important for transparency and for handling any petitions or disputes.

Audit Log

The Audit Log shows every ballot event in the order it occurred. Each entry contains:

Audit Field Description
Timestamp The exact date and time the ballot was cast (to the millisecond)
Ballot Hash A unique cryptographic fingerprint of this specific ballot
Previous Hash The hash of the ballot before this one — forming an unbreakable chain
Voter Token An anonymised token (not the student's matric number) proving this was a valid accredited voter
Chain Integrity A verified/failed indicator showing whether the ballot chain is intact

Downloading Records

Use the Export buttons on the Results and Voter Roll pages to download CSV files of final results and voter participation records. Store these securely as your official electoral archive for the session.

📄
Record Retention

Keep your downloaded results and audit exports for a minimum of one full academic session. These may be required by your institution's Student Affairs office or in the event of an electoral petition.

Troubleshooting Common Issues

Issue Likely Cause Solution
Cannot log in Wrong password or email Try the exact credentials provided. Contact your platform administrator for a reset.
Dashboard shows no data No students uploaded yet Upload your voter roll Excel file under Voter Roll → Upload Roster.
Election won't go LIVE License is not ACTIVE Contact your StudElect platform representative. A payment hold may be blocking activation.
Student gets "Not found" at voter booth Student not on voter roll Check their matric number carefully. Add them manually if they are eligible.
Candidate not appearing on ballot Candidate screening status is not "Cleared" Go to Candidates, find the candidate, change status to Cleared.
Results page shows 0 votes Results are hidden Go to Election Settings and toggle Results Visibility to Public.
Student voted twice error They already cast a ballot The system enforces one-vote-per-matric. Check the audit log for confirmation. This cannot be reversed.
Excel upload fails File format or structure issue Ensure it is a .xlsx file (not .xls or .csv). Check that row 1 is headers and data starts at row 2. Remove merged cells.
Voter booth is very slow High simultaneous traffic The platform auto-scales. Advise students to try again in a moment. Stagger announcements so not all students rush to vote at once.

Contact Support

If you encounter an issue not covered here, contact your StudElect platform representative via WhatsApp. Always provide:

  • Your institution and organisation name
  • The exact error message or screenshot
  • The approximate time the issue occurred
  • The matric number of the affected student (if applicable)
